## Speakers (short)

- **Sumeet Motwani**: Oxford ML PhD (advisors Philip Torr, Christian Schroeder de Witt); RL post-training, multi-agent systems, AI security; lead author of "Secret Collusion among AI Agents: Multi-Agent Deception via Steganography" (NeurIPS 2024).
- **Joan Velja**: Oxford PhD (supervisor Alessandro Abate, funded by the Alignment Project); scalable oversight; co-author of "Hidden in Plain Text" (AACL 2025, oral).
- **Eugene Bagdasarian**: Assistant Professor, UMass Amherst; co-leads the AI Security Lab and AI Safety Initiative; part-time senior research scientist at Google; Colosseum; USENIX Security 2024 Distinguished Paper Award.
- **Mason Nakamura**: 4th-year PhD, UMass Amherst (Resource-Bounded Reasoning Lab, Shlomo Zilberstein); NSF GRF; multi-agent safety.
- **Usman Anwar**: final-year PhD, Cambridge (David Krueger); CoT monitorability, detecting steganography; lead author of "Foundational Challenges in Assuring Alignment and Safety of LLMs" (TMLR 2024).
- **Matthew Lee**: Head of Development, Poseidon Research (NYC lab on deception and hidden information in AI; steganography model organisms, action-based steganography, black/white-box defences).
- **Jerick Shi**: Software Engineer, The Voleon Group; CMU MSCS 2026; thesis on deceptive behaviour in multi-agent LLM systems (co-advised by Vincent Conitzer and Zhijing Jin).
- **Jurgis Kemeklis**: MIT undergraduate, MIT AI Alignment board; works with Redwood Research on simulation-based control and red-teaming monitors; 1st place at Apart's AI Control Hackathon 2026 ("Omission Attacks").
- **Khanh Linh Nguyen**: Head of AI/ML at Obello, Google Developer Expert in AI/ML, AI Safety Vietnam; lead author of TraceGuard.
