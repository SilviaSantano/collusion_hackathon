## Key facts at a glance

- **Dates:** Fri 23 – Sun 25 Oct 2026. **Deadline: Sun 25 Oct, 11:59 PM AoE.**
- **Format:** online (Discord + Zoom); in-person hubs NYC (Collider) and Hong Kong on Sat–Sun, plus community hubs.
- **Teams:** 1–5 recommended (larger allowed); each person on at most two projects.
- **Prizes:** $2,000 total: $1,000 / $500 / $300 / $100 / $100. Top teams fast-tracked to apply to the Apart Fellowship.
- **Modal credits:** $250 per person at the NYC hub; $50 for every other participant.
- **Report:** PDF on the official template, ≤ 8 pages excl. references and appendices, abstract 150–250 words, required Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical appendix.
- **Rubric:** Impact & Innovation, Execution Quality, Presentation & Clarity (1–5 each). Same rubric across tracks.
- **Starter repo:** https://github.com/poseidon-research/collusion_hackathon (optional; this fork).
- **Support:** Discord help-desk channel (tag @Support), sprints@apartresearch.com. Discord: discord.gg/XswWBvugYs
- **Sign-ups:** 1,575 at 23 Oct.
